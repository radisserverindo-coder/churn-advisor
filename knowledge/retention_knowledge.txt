TELECOM CUSTOMER RETENTION KNOWLEDGE BASE

1. MONTH-TO-MONTH CONTRACT

Customers with month-to-month contracts generally have higher churn risk because they have greater flexibility to leave the service.

Recommended retention strategies:
- Offer a one-year contract with an appropriate discount.
- Explain the benefits of a longer contract.
- Provide personalized retention offers.
- Consider a temporary monthly credit for high-risk customers.

2. FIBER OPTIC CUSTOMERS

Fiber optic customers can have high monthly charges and may require additional technical support.

Recommended retention strategies:
- Offer free technical support.
- Check whether the customer is experiencing connection problems.
- Provide service-quality assistance.
- Consider a loyalty discount for high-risk customers.

3. HIGH CHURN RISK

A customer with a high predicted churn probability should receive attention from the retention team.

Recommended actions:
- Contact the customer proactively.
- Review the customer's contract type.
- Review monthly charges.
- Identify service-related problems.
- Offer a targeted retention package.

4. MEDIUM CHURN RISK

Medium-risk customers should be monitored and may receive preventive retention offers.

Recommended actions:
- Monitor changes in customer behavior.
- Provide personalized communication.
- Consider a moderate discount.
- Encourage longer-term contracts.

5. LOW CHURN RISK

Low-risk customers generally do not require aggressive retention offers.

Recommended actions:
- Maintain good service quality.
- Continue normal customer engagement.
- Avoid unnecessary discounts.

6. RETENTION OFFER PRINCIPLES

Retention offers should be targeted rather than automatically applied to every customer.

The retention team should consider:
- Churn probability.
- Contract type.
- Monthly charges.
- Internet service.
- Customer tenure.
- Available retention budget.

The goal is to reduce churn while avoiding unnecessary discounts for customers who are unlikely to leave.

7. CONTRACT STRATEGY

A one-year contract can be considered for customers currently using month-to-month contracts.

A two-year contract can provide stronger long-term retention but may require a larger incentive.

The appropriate offer should depend on the customer's predicted risk and current characteristics.

8. AI ADVISOR

The AI advisor should provide recommendations based on the customer's churn prediction and the retention knowledge base.

The AI should not invent company policies that are not present in the knowledge base.

When the user's question does not require the knowledge base, the system should avoid unnecessary retrieval to reduce token usage.

When the question requires retention strategy information, the system should retrieve only the most relevant knowledge chunks.
